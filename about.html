MY STORY

Every developer has a starting point.

Mine started with curiosity and grew into a passion for creating things with code.

My journey into web development started with curiosity about how websites work and how ideas are transformed into interactive experiences.

As my understanding grew, I became interested in what happens behind the scenes. This led me into backend development, where I explored Node.js, Express and MongoDB through practical learning and courses by John Smilga.
